**Format:** Voiceover + on-screen visuals/screenshots

## Storyboard (Scene-by-Scene)

### Scene 1: Hook (0:00–0:05)
- **Visual:** Inertia logo, minimal background (e.g. subtle gradient or brand green)
- **Text overlay:** *"Professional Portfolio Management"*
- **Voiceover:** *"Your investments deserve professional care."*

### Scene 2: Portfolio & Client Management (0:05–0:15)
- **Visual:** Dashboard or client portfolio view (anonymised)
- **Key elements:** Holdings list, portfolio value, asset allocation chart
- **Voiceover:** *"At Inertia, we use enterprise-grade technology to manage your portfolio — real-time tracking, accurate holdings, and transparent performance."*

### Scene 3: Workflow (0:15–0:25)
- **Visual:** Workflow pipeline: FUNDS → RECOS → NOTIFY → EXEC → UPDATE → COMPLETED
- **Key elements:** Stage icons, status labels, arrows
- **Voiceover:** *"Every investment follows a clear process: from fund receipt to recommendations, client approval, and execution. Nothing falls through the cracks."*

### Scene 4: Data Integrity (0:25–0:35)
- **Visual:** Data integrity / monitoring dashboard
- **Key elements:** Validation checks, SLA metrics, issue resolution
- **Voiceover:** *"We monitor data quality and service levels automatically. Your portfolio data is validated and issues are flagged and resolved promptly."*

### Scene 5: AI & Recommendations (0:35–0:45)
- **Visual:** Recommendation screen or asset allocation view
- **Key elements:** Risk profile, suggested allocation, securities
- **Voiceover:** *"Our AI-powered recommendations are tailored to your risk profile and goals. Asset allocation is systematic and aligned with your financial plan."*

### Scene 6: Communication (0:45–0:55)
- **Visual:** Email / WhatsApp / review icons
- **Key elements:** Notification icons, “You’re always informed” style line
- **Voiceover:** *"You stay informed. Email updates, WhatsApp notifications, and scheduled reviews keep you in the loop every step of the way."*

### Scene 7: Closing (0:55–1:00)
- **Visual:** Logo + tagline
- **Text overlay:** *"INERTIA Equities For Wealth"*
- **Voiceover:** *"Inertia — Professional portfolio management, powered by discipline and technology."*

---

## Production Notes

- **Tools:** Canva, Loom, or professional video editing software (e.g. Premiere, DaVinci Resolve)
- **Voiceover:** Clear, professional tone; pace ~140–150 words per minute
- **Background music:** Optional; keep low volume if used
- **Screen recordings:** Use anonymised or demo data only
- **Branding:** Use Inertia green (#155724) and logo consistently
