NSE file layout

Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or CSV/TSV. Dividend, bonus, and split are calculated from PURPOSE — extra columns are ignored.

  • SYMBOL — required
  • EX-DATE — required, DD-MMM-YYYY (e.g. 03-Nov-2025)
  • PURPOSE — required for parsing
  • FACE VALUE — used for % dividends (optional)
  • COMPANY NAME, SERIES, RECORD DATE, book-closure dates — optional

PURPOSE examples

  • Interim Dividend - Rs 11 Per Share / Re 1 Per Share
  • Face Value Split (Sub-Division) - From Rs 10/- Per Share To Rs 5/- Per Share
  • Bonus 1:3 / Bonus Issue 1:1 — stored as extra shares per share held (1:3 → 0.333, 1:1 → 1)
  • Distribution - Rs 3.60 Per Unit Consisting Of Interest / Dividend / Return Of Capital (InvIT/REIT — full amount as dividend; PURPOSE keeps the breakdown)

Held names appear first and are pre-selected. Other names in the securities master are listed as “Not held” — tick them to import. Gilt “Interest Payment” (SERIES GS) is listed but not imported.
