Workflow Lifecycle Information
Workflow Stages:
  • FUNDS - Funds Received
  • RECOS - Recommendations Generated
  • NOTIFY - Client Notified
  • EXEC - Executed
  • UPDATE - Portfolio Updated
  • COMPLETED - Completed
Monthly Cycle Process:
  1. Month Start: Create new monthly investments for recurring clients
  2. Throughout Month: Process workflows through stages
  3. Month End: Generate monthly reports
  4. Cleanup: Archive old completed workflows (30+ days)
  5. Next Month: Repeat cycle
Best Practices:
  • Archive workflows older than 30 days to keep the system performant
  • Run monthly reports before archiving to preserve data
  • Prepare next month cycle at the beginning of each month
  • Monitor workflow progress through the daily workflow report
