Trade edit — cashflow review

What to do after changing a BUY/SELL trade

Back

Trades and cashflows are separate. Adding a new trade still writes a cashflow line. Editing a trade updates holdings only — cashflow rows stay as they are. After an edit, open Cashflows (detail view) and use Edit on the matching row.

Checklist after you save a trade
  1. Note the client and trade date.
  2. Open Cashflows for that client (detail view) and use Edit on the matching row.
  3. Find matching rows — advisor notes, BUY … shares of …, or Net cashflow for ….
  4. Update amounts so they match the corrected trade:
    • BUY — money into portfolio (typically negative / INFLOW)
    • SELL — proceeds out (typically positive / OUTFLOW)
  5. If the date changed, check cashflows on both the old and new dates.
  6. Optional: use the client’s Cashflow ↔ trades playbook to verify.
Same day, multiple cashflows

You may have more than one cashflow on the same calendar day (split advisor notes). Use the cashflows detail view to edit the specific line — not only the netted summary.

Do not assume
  • Holdings may update while cashflow stays wrong.
  • XIRR and period reports use cashflows — fix cashflow before trusting performance.
