How Data Integrity Manager works

  • Creates issues (not alerts) for: Recommendation↔Trade matching, Cashflows, Duplicates, Date integrity, Negative values.
  • Skips checks: Holdings vs Transactions and Corporate Actions (handled by forward calc in your setup).
  • Learning: user feedback can create one-time exceptions (`DataIntegrityException`) or client patterns (`ClientBehaviorPattern`).
  • Resolution: resolving issues closes them; the orchestrator later decides if a consolidated client alert is needed.
