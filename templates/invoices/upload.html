Back to invoices

Step 1 of 2: Upload a Zoho Books export (.csv, .tsv, .xls, or .xlsx — first sheet is read for Excel). You will then confirm each row by matching the file customer name to a client in the database. Rows whose invoice number is already in the database or repeated in the file are skipped (no duplicates). On the confirm screen you can download skipped or invalid rows as CSV; after import, any commit failures can be downloaded from the invoice list.

Required / used columns
  • Invoice Number — unique; duplicates skipped
  • Invoice Date (or Issued Date)
  • Due Date — falls back to invoice date
  • Total, SubTotal
  • Invoice Status — maps to draft/sent/paid/overdue/cancelled
  • Customer Name and/or Primary Contact EmailID — must match a client you can access
Optional (stored in notes or amounts)
  • Invoice ID — reference line in notes
  • Notes, Item Desc / Item Name
  • Balance — affects status when Closed
  • Item Tax1 Amount — or Total − SubTotal for tax
  • Last Payment Date / Expected Payment Date — paid date when status is paid
Agreement. If a client has no signed/active agreement yet, the invoice is still imported and agreement is left blank until you add one. After import, the invoice list shows everyone who was missing an agreement. Imported invoices have no line items (header-level only). Billing period start/end are set to the invoice date.
