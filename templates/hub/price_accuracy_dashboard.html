Price data accuracy

Scans historical_price for jumps, zero closes, and holes longer than 5 open days (weekdays that are not holidays). A single missing day is ignored — valuation already uses a close a few days either side. Saturday/Sunday are never flagged. If names that traded that weekday all have no close, confirm it as a market holiday below. Days with no trade are ignored — valuation already uses a nearby close. If some names have a close that day, the market was open (a 54-of-105 hole is missing history, not a holiday). A jump that matches a split or bonus on file (e.g. RELIANCE −50%, HDFCBANK −75%) is not listed — stored closes are as-traded, not split-adjusted. The Prices tab update does not backfill old missing dates — use the Sheets queue or IST close historical save for that. Once a jump is confirmed (UI or Google Apps Script), it is stored and skipped on later runs.

Verify from Google Sheet fetches GOOGLEFINANCE closes on the two historical dates (HistoricalPrices worksheet), not the Prices tab (that tab only updates today’s last price, and historical rows save at IST close). A spike is auto-closed if rupee levels match within {{ sheet_tolerance_pct }}% or the % move matches (GOOGLEFINANCE is often split-adjusted, so levels can differ). Real data errors — sheet move does not match the DB jump — stay open.
Import rule: do not write GOOGLEFINANCE closes into history when they diverge from as-traded neighbors after a later split/bonus — apply-prices and missing-fill reject those and require unadjusted NSE closes.
